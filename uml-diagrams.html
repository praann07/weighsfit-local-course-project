Database Design & Normalization: ER Diagram

Account & progressWorkoutNutrition- - -  optional created_by link (custom exercises / foods)
  • 1:1 split: profiles holds personal attributes separately from the login identity in users.
  • M:N resolved: routine_exercises (routines ↔ exercises) and food_logs (users ↔ foods) are junction tables that carry their own attributes.
  • 3NF: exercise and food facts (muscle group, macros per 100 g) are stored once and referenced by foreign key, not repeated in every set or log.
  • Deliberate denormalization: routine_snapshot (JSON) freezes a routine at session start so later edits never rewrite history.
